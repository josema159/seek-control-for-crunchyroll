--- Name ---
Seek Control for Crunchyroll

--- Short description / Summary (max 132 characters on Chrome) ---
Choose how far the arrow keys jump on Crunchyroll, and add precise 1-second jumps with the , . and < > keys.

--- Detailed description (first 250 characters carry the key points) ---
Choose how far the arrow keys jump on Crunchyroll (default 5 s) and add precise jumps with the , . or < > keys (default 1 s). Both values go from 0.1 to 120 seconds, apply instantly without reloading, and the extension collects no data.

FEATURES
• Arrow keys ← → jump the distance you choose (default: 5 seconds).
• Fine jumps with , and . — or with < and > — (default: 1 second). Perfect for catching a line of dialogue you missed or finding the exact moment.
• Works with US keyboards (, . or Shift + , .) and Spanish/Latin American keyboards (the dedicated <> key).
• Any value from 0.1 to 120 seconds for each.
• Changes apply instantly, no need to reload the episode.
• Shortcuts are ignored while you type in search or comment boxes.
• English and Spanish interface.

HOW TO USE
1. Open an episode on Crunchyroll.
2. Click the extension icon to set your jump durations, then press Save.
3. Use the arrow keys or the , . < > keys while watching.

PRIVACY
The extension stores only your two jump settings, locally in your browser. It collects no data, uses no tracking or analytics, and makes no network requests.

This extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Crunchyroll.
